How to play

Collect blocks and use them to build your world!

1. select a tool

  • Axe: Collect tree blocks.
  • Pickaxe: Collect rock blocks.
  • Shovel: Collect dirt blocks.

2. Collect blocks

Select a tool, then click a matching block in the world. The block will be removed and added to your inventory.

3. Place blocks

Select a collected block from your inventory. Then click an empty space in the world to place it.

4. Reset the game

Click Reset to restore the starting world and clear your inventory.
